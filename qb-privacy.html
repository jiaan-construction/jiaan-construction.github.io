Privacy Policy

This policy explains how JiaAn QuickBooks Bridge accesses, uses, stores, and shares Intuit user data.

1. Data the app accesses

The app uses Intuit's com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting scope. After an authorized administrator grants consent, it may access:

  • the connected company's name, legal name, country, fiscal year start, and company identifier;
  • customers and their contact details, addresses, and balances;
  • invoices, payments, and other accounting transactions;
  • standard financial reports, including profit and loss, balance sheet, aged receivables, and income by customer.

2. How data is used

Company data is used only to answer the operator's own lookups, reports, and reconciliation checks against that same company's records. It is not used for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, resale, or any unrelated purpose.

3. Storage and retention

  • OAuth credentials and the refresh token are stored in a permission-restricted file on an operator-controlled local computer.
  • The app has no server component and maintains no remote copy of the accounting data.
  • Report and query results are held in memory for the duration of a command and are written to disk only when the operator explicitly saves them.
  • OAuth credentials remain until they are revoked or the local file is deleted.

4. AI-assisted processing and sharing

When an authorized operator explicitly requests analysis or summarization, the retrieved company data may be processed by the AI assistant service used by the operator solely to complete that request. Intuit data is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared for unrelated purposes.

5. Security

The app uses Intuit's OAuth 2.0 flow and never collects or stores Intuit passwords. Access is limited to a company that completes Intuit's consent process. All read operations are read-only. The single write operation, creating a customer record, requires an explicit confirmation flag on each invocation and defaults to printing the intended record without writing.

6. User control

An administrator may revoke access at any time from the QuickBooks Online company's connected-apps settings. They may also request deletion of locally stored OAuth credentials by contacting the operator. See disconnect for the steps.
