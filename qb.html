JiaAn QuickBooks Bridge

A private command-line utility used by JIA AN Construction Pte. Ltd. to read its own QuickBooks Online accounting data from an operator-controlled computer.

Limited purpose. This tool is not offered as a public or multi-tenant service. It connects to a single QuickBooks Online company, and only after that company's own administrator completes Intuit's OAuth consent flow.

What the tool does

  • Confirms which QuickBooks Online company is connected.
  • Runs standard reports — profit and loss, balance sheet, aged receivables, customer income — for a date range chosen by the operator.
  • Lists customers and invoices, and runs read-only queries requested by the operator.
  • Creates a customer record only when the operator passes an explicit confirmation flag; without it the command prints the intended record and writes nothing.

What the tool cannot do

  • It cannot move money, record or alter payments, or submit any tax filing.
  • It cannot delete accounts, customers, invoices, or transactions.
  • It cannot change company settings, the chart of accounts, or existing journal entries.
  • It does not connect to any company other than the one whose administrator authorized it.

Data handling

The app requests the com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting scope. OAuth credentials and the refresh token are stored in a permission-restricted file on an operator-controlled local computer. There is no server component and no remote copy of the accounting data. Company data is retrieved only in response to an operator command. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Connecting and disconnecting

To connect, the operator runs the local authorization command and completes Intuit's consent screen — see connect. To disconnect, revoke the app from the QuickBooks Online company's connected-apps settings and delete the local token file — see disconnect.
