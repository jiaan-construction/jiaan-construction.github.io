Disconnect

How an authorized administrator disconnects a QuickBooks Online company from JiaAn QuickBooks Bridge.

Disconnecting

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Settings → Apps → Connected apps and disconnect JiaAn QuickBooks Bridge. This revokes the refresh token at Intuit immediately.
  2. Delete the local token file on the operator-controlled computer to remove the stored credentials.

After either step the tool can no longer read company data. Because the app keeps no server-side copy of accounting data, revoking access leaves nothing to delete remotely. To request confirmation of deletion, contact the operator.
